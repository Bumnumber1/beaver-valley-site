Skunk

Liquid-waste tanker

  • Top speed 17 kn
  • Hull 200 HP
  • Crew posts 9
  • Cargo 600 t
  • Armament Unarmed
  • Uses terminal pumps to load and unload sewage.
  • Traffic variants Cistern carries 700 t of fuel. Skunk 1 and Skunk 2 serve the east and west sewer berths with 600 t capacity. These merchant contacts have 100 HP, top speed 31 kn, route cruise 16 kn, and dimensions of 110 × 20 × 5 m.

Ox

  • Top speed 24 kn
  • Hull 420 HP
  • Crew posts 4
  • Open cargo deck 138 m
  • Armament Unarmed
  • Two deck cranes handle heavy cargo. The Angler deep submersible rides on its deck.
  • Merchant variants Ironfin carries 500 t of steel or food. Mercy carries 250 t of machinery or medicine. Both have 100 HP, a 31-knot rating, a 16-knot route cruise, and a 110 × 20 × 5 m merchant hull.
  • Where to find it Mission selection.
