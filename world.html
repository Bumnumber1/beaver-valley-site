Downtown & commerce

Shops and city streets

FoundryStop, McDoogles and the casino occupy different parts of town. Walk inside the shops to reach their counters. The casino’s poker room has its own table and a separate main-menu setup for a custom game.

Places to live

Homes above and below the skyline

A resident’s home can be different from the place they start the day. The Dumpster Talk host and ByteBinge have separate homes off the same landing. A home stash carries items across visits. In the slums, sleeping bags and stash spots serve the same household needs. General cooking is still in development.

Law & custody

From the precinct to the cell block

Chief Jesse Ventura and intake officer Biden start at the police station. Other officers have patrol, home or helicopter assignments. Municipal custody and the American Security Services detention complex are separate places. The latter has its own intake, holding wings and support rooms.

The graveyard & the underground

From the church to the crypt

The Kitchen Crypt is home to the Zombie Chefs. Completing the shrimp recipe can send Tyrone across town. Eating The Delicacy at the graveyard starts a performance there. The chefs are silent, and surviving unrecruited performers can remain in the graveyard afterward. Beneath the surface, the sewer routes pass through their own rooms, channels and hazards.

Venues & shows

On stage and behind the scenes

At James’s Barcade, Beaver Valley Pinball shares the room with Sewer Pipe, Oregon River and Sewer Beaver. The theatre has a lobby, audience seating and a lighting booth. The bar is both a Story Mode venue and a place for eligible Beaver Machine members to play a set.

Air & ground travel

From Beaver Valley to Moscow

Beaver Valley Air has separate passenger, cargo and cockpit spaces. The Washington and Moscow destinations have their own arrival scenes. Sandy Shores opens through its own campaign progression.

War & the forward base

The front and the Grand FOB

War takes the squad out of the base and onto battlefields with different terrain. Back at the FOB, the briefing room, armory, infirmary, detention block and quartermaster each have a distinct job.

Harbors & ships

Walk aboard the fleet

The ocean chart and the rooms aboard a vessel are different views of the voyage. A frigate has a bridge and a flight deck. A submarine has a control room, sonar room and engineering spaces. Civilian ships have their own interiors, including the ferry passenger lounge and the Muskrat’s working deck.
