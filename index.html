Story Mode

A day in the Valley

Beaver Valley is a satirical role-playing game with side-scrolling, physics-driven fights. On the street, lining up your feet matters as much as reaching your opponent. A fight can carry through a doorway, and a witness can turn a scuffle into a police case.

The town has a two-hour timetable. A resident can leave work, visit a public venue and head home through the same doors you use. Recruit someone and their place in your party takes precedence over their routine. At home you can use a personal stash or rest to pass time and recover health.

Music & performance

Beaver Machine Live

Choose vocals, guitar, bass or drums. All four parts use a four-lane note highway, with taps, sustained notes and a combo multiplier. Start with a single song, then try a full set. The songbook also accepts your own music.

The game scores timing, accuracy and the notes you hold. Play from the main menu, or take a band member’s part at the bar. In the venue, the musicians and audience share the screen with your chart.

Ocean Operations

Naval combat

In The Last Ferry, the passenger ship is the objective. Pick a side and the available commands change with the mission. On a bridge, you can follow reported contacts on the chart, give another friendly vessel an order or switch to a shipboard station.

Other operations put you in a submarine or on a carrier. Aircraft, boarding parties and damage-control crews each have jobs to do. The mission editor lets you set starting forces and decide what counts as a win.

From the main menu

Story Mode

Choose a resident and explore on foot. Homes, jobs, travel, recruitment and encounters share the same world.

VS Mode

Set up opposing fighters with human or AI control and take them into an arena.

War

Assemble a squad, choose a battlefield and return to the Grand FOB for supplies, treatment and the next assignment.

Court Mode

Choose a cast for a trial. Testimony, arguments and the jury’s decision play out in court.

Murder Mystery

Investigate a death at Bellweather House. Examine physical evidence, talk to suspects and compare their accounts in your notebook.

Telethon

Run a debate show with speaking hosts. Connecting Twitch brings live chat into the broadcast.

Beaver Slap

Build a deck and play for control of spaces on the board with character, item and location cards.

Poker

Choose the players, buy-in and blinds for a game of Texas hold’em with human or computer-controlled seats.

Beaver Machine Live

Play a four-lane chart for vocals, guitar, bass or drums. Choose your difficulty and take a song or set onstage.

Once in town, you can also train at Namasensei’s dojo, play the cabinets at James’s Barcade, catch a theatre performance, or join a show at the UNSE Club.

Bellweather House

A case you can investigate

The investigator enters after the opening crime. Guests know different things about the people and rooms they saw. Keep track of their statements, the body and the physical evidence before naming a suspect.
